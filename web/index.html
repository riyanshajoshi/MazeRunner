HOW TO PLAY

Escape the house.

  1. Explore the same daily maze as every other player.
  2. Find and take the Brass Key.
  3. Reach the locked Vault to escape.
  4. Every second successful move lets The Watcher move one step toward you using BFS.
  5. You have a limited number of moves. Share your spoiler-free result with friends.

The map layout stays faithful to the Java game: rooms are graph nodes and doors are graph edges.
